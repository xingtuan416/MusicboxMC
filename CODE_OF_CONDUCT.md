# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. This is a hobby project about turning music into
Minecraft redstone, and there is no reason for it to be unpleasant.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about a timing convention or a
  blockstate detail, not about anyone's competence.
- Critique the code, not the person. "This will break when the track is longer
  than 256 cells, here is the case" is useful. "This is wrong" is not.
- Be patient with people who are new to the project, the language, or Minecraft
  redstone. Explaining a domain rule costs you a paragraph.
- Accept that the maintainer may say no. Scope is a real constraint on a project
  one person works on in their spare time, and "not now" is not a judgement of
  your idea.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory comments about anyone's background, identity or experience.
- Sexual attention or imagery in any project space.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or repeatedly reopening a settled decision.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other space where
you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/xingtuan416/MusicboxMC/security/advisories/new)
or by emailing the maintainer. Reports are handled privately.

Depending on severity, the response may be a request to stop, a comment removed,
or a block from the repository. The maintainer's decision is final, but you can
always ask for it to be reconsidered.
